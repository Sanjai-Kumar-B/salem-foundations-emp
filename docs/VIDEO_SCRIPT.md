# Salem Foundations Admissions Portal — Video Narration Script

**Target Video Length**: 8 to 10 Minutes  
**Tone**: Professional, encouraging, clear, and institutional  
**Audience**: Admissions Directors, Admission Counsellors, and Institutional Stakeholders  

---

## Scene 1: Introduction & Universal Authentication
- **Visual**: `01-login-page.png` — Splash screen transition to the dual-role sign-in portal.
- **Duration**: 45 Seconds
- **Narration**:
  > *"Welcome to the Salem Foundations Admissions and Telecalling Portal — the unified platform engineered to streamline institutional admissions and empower educational telecallers.*  
  > *Accessing the system is straightforward. Enter your institutional email address and password on the universal login screen. The platform automatically determines your role — seamlessly directing administrators to the Executive Control Suite, and academic counsellors directly to their calling desk.*  
  > *Role-based security guards ensure student data remains confidential, protected by enterprise Firebase authentication."*

---

## Scene 2: Administrator Executive Dashboard
- **Visual**: `03-admin-dashboard.png` — Executive Dashboard KPI cards and pipeline funnel.
- **Duration**: 45 Seconds
- **Narration**:
  > *"For admissions leadership, the Executive Dashboard delivers an instantaneous, live pulse of your entire campaign.*  
  > *At the top, high-visibility metric cards showcase total student inquiries, assigned versus unassigned ratios, high-priority prospects, and real-time call tallies for today.*  
  > *Scroll down to inspect the interactive Admissions Funnel, tracking conversion velocity from raw inquiry to confirmed enrollment. Below, the staff productivity leaderboard provides full transparency into counsellor activity, talk durations, and target achievements."*

---

## Scene 3: Lead Ingestion & Duplicate Detection
- **Visual**: `04-admin-leads.png` transitioning to `05-admin-leads-import-modal.png` — Lead directory and bulk file uploader.
- **Duration**: 50 Seconds
- **Narration**:
  > *"Importing student inquiries from school outreach, education expos, or digital campaigns takes only seconds.*  
  > *Click 'Import Leads' and simply drag and drop your spreadsheet — supporting both CSV and Excel formats. Our intelligent parser automatically maps student names, contact numbers, course preferences, and districts.*  
  > *Crucially, the system features automated duplicate detection. If a phone number already exists in the registry, the system flags it instantly for administrator review. With a single click, discard duplicates to keep your database pristine, or manually add individual inquiries with custom priorities."*

---

## Scene 4: Intelligent Leads Assignment Center
- **Visual**: `06-admin-assignments.png` — Split-pane assignment console with team workload average.
- **Duration**: 50 Seconds
- **Narration**:
  > *"Distributing leads equitably is vital for team morale and conversion speed. The Leads Assignment Center introduces a dual-pane allocation workflow.*  
  > *On the left, filter and batch-select student candidates. On the right, select a single counsellor to receive the batch.*  
  > *Notice the dynamic workload status badges: 'Available', 'Balanced', or 'Overloaded'. Calculated against the institutional team average, this prevents staff burnout by ensuring no single counsellor is overburdened while others sit idle.*  
  > *The Assignment History tab allows administrators to trace historical allocations and effortlessly reassign leads when needed."*

---

## Scene 5: Employee Roster & Staff Management
- **Visual**: `07-admin-employees.png` — Employee roster and staff profile drawer.
- **Duration**: 45 Seconds
- **Narration**:
  > *"Managing your admissions staff is centralized under Employee Management.*  
  > *Admins can onboard new team members with complete records — including Date of Birth, Joining Date, and contact details.*  
  > *Calling targets are workload-adaptive, dynamically scaling with assigned student volume rather than arbitrary quotas.*  
  > *Toggle staff availability between 'Active' and 'Deactivate' with a single click. When an employee departs, our safe cascading removal safely returns their assigned students back into the unassigned pool, guaranteeing zero lost opportunities."*

---

## Scene 6: Analytics & Branded Institutional PDF Reports
- **Visual**: `08-admin-analytics.png` — Analytics charts and downloadable PDF preview.
- **Duration**: 55 Seconds
- **Narration**:
  > *"Under Analytics, transform raw telecalling activity into strategic institutional intelligence.*  
  > *Filter by today, yesterday, the past week, or past month to observe conversion drop-offs, lead source attribution, and staff talk-time leaderboards.*  
  > *Need to brief the governing board or trustees? Click 'Download PDF Report'. The portal instantly renders an executive, multi-page vector PDF featuring the Salem Foundations crest, the Oddmachine technology badge, a subtle security watermark, and formatted data tables ready for print or presentation."*

---

## Scene 7: WhatsApp Template Authoring
- **Visual**: Admin WhatsApp manager (`/admin/whatsapp`) showing template cards and editor modal.
- **Duration**: 45 Seconds
- **Narration**:
  > *"Engaging prospective students on their preferred channel is essential. Under WhatsApp Templates, administrators curate official collateral templates.*  
  > *Create templates for course brochures, fee structures, and campus visit invitations. Use dynamic variable tags like {name}, {course}, and {counsellorName}.*  
  > *When enabled, these templates sync immediately to the telecallers' consoles, empowering them to deliver standardized, branded collateral in a single click."*

---

## Scene 8: Counsellor Dashboard & Daily Schedule
- **Visual**: `10-counsellor-dashboard.png` and `12-counsellor-todays-calls.png` — Counsellor metrics and scheduled calls.
- **Duration**: 45 Seconds
- **Narration**:
  > *"Now let's step into the counsellor's experience.*  
  > *Upon sign-in, counsellors see their daily performance progress bar, workload target, and urgent task counters.*  
  > *Under 'Schedule', calls are cleanly categorized: 'Overdue' callbacks are highlighted in bold red so urgent commitments are never forgotten, while 'Upcoming Today' displays scheduled appointment slots.*  
  > *Clicking 'Start Telecalling' launches the counsellor directly into their priority queue."*

---

## Scene 9: Telecalling Workspace & Live Stopwatch
- **Visual**: `11-counsellor-workspace.png` — Candidate card, auto-dialer trigger, and active call timer.
- **Duration**: 50 Seconds
- **Narration**:
  > *"The Telecalling Workspace is built for speed and focus.*  
  > *Our Next-Best-Action engine automatically serves the most critical student next — prioritizing overdue callbacks first, today's appointments second, and high-priority fresh leads third.*  
  > *Review the student's program preference and previous notes. Click 'Call Student Now' to launch your phone or softphone dialer.*  
  > *Once connected, the live stopwatch tracks duration while keeping vital course facts front and center. When the discussion finishes, click 'End Call & Record Outcome'."*

---

## Scene 10: Standardized Outcome & Optional WhatsApp Collateral
- **Visual**: Post-call disposition screen showing strict outcome options, follow-up calendar, and WhatsApp preview.
- **Duration**: 55 Seconds
- **Narration**:
  > *"Every conversation concludes with a standardized disposition.*  
  > *Counsellors select from six strict outcomes. Selecting 'Interested' or 'Call Later' mandates scheduling a follow-up using quick presets or custom calendar slots, followed by discussion notes.*  
  > *Directly below, counsellors have the option to send follow-up WhatsApp collateral. Selecting an admin template generates an instant personalized preview with the student's actual name and course pre-filled.*  
  > *Click 'Send via WhatsApp' to open the chat instantly, or click 'Save Outcome & Load Next' — which automatically opens WhatsApp while seamlessly queuing the next student."*

---

## Scene 11: Dedicated Profile & Session Sign-Out
- **Visual**: Profile view showing staff identity and dedicated Sign Out button.
- **Duration**: 30 Seconds
- **Narration**:
  > *"Both administrators and counsellors manage their credentials through a dedicated Profile screen.*  
  > *Review your employee details, assigned department, and performance metrics. To safely end your work shift and protect student privacy, click the prominent red 'Sign Out' button.*  
  > *Thank you for touring the Salem Foundations Admissions and Telecalling Portal — driving excellence in higher education enrollment."*
